Pekka gets a bottle-return receipt for six euros and ten cents. His written estimate says six thirty. The missing twenty cents is one foreign bottle, so he carries it into the supermarket as evidence. At self-checkout, the anti-theft camera sees a bottle leaving without being scanned. Pekka explains that it entered without being accepted. The assistant looks at the bottle, then at Pekka, and waves him through. Somebody finally understands the scale of the problem.
